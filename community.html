01 ABOUT VORVENA

BUILT FOR PEOPLE WHO CAN CREATE.

VORVENA is a community designed to help skilled professionals showcase their abilities and connect with clients who need their services.

From designers and developers to photographers, writers, marketers and other professionals, VORVENA gives talented people a place to be discovered.

03 FOR CLIENTS

FIND THE RIGHT PERSON FOR THE JOB.

Clients can explore approved professional profiles and discover people whose skills match their project needs.

Clients only see the professional information that the worker has chosen to make public. Private information such as phone numbers, email addresses and uploaded CVs remain protected.

04 TRUST & VERIFICATION

EVERY PROFILE MATTERS.

Joining VORVENA does not automatically place a professional on the public community.

Applications are reviewed by administrators. Applicants may be asked to provide proof of their skills through a CV, portfolio, work samples or other professional information.
