PRIVACY POLICY.

Your information matters. This policy explains what information VORVENA collects, why we collect it and how we protect it.

Last Updated: August 2026

ON THIS PAGE

01

Information We Collect

When you use VORVENA, we may collect information that you provide directly to us.

  • Name and contact information
  • Profile picture
  • Professional information
  • Skills and experience
  • CV and work samples
  • Portfolio links
  • Account login information
  • Information submitted through forms and communications
02

How Information Is Collected

Information may be collected when you create an account, apply to join the community, update your profile, submit a project request, contact VORVENA or otherwise interact with the platform.

We may also automatically collect limited technical information required to operate and secure the platform.

03

How We Use Your Information

VORVENA may use collected information to:

  • Review community applications
  • Create and manage user accounts
  • Create professional profiles
  • Connect professionals with clients
  • Communicate about projects and opportunities
  • Improve platform functionality
  • Prevent fraud and abuse
  • Maintain platform security
04

Public Professional Profiles

When a professional is approved, VORVENA may create a public profile containing selected professional information.

This may include the professional's name, profile picture, profession, skills, experience, biography and selected portfolio information.

IMPORTANT

Private application information will not automatically become public simply because a member is approved.

05

CVs & Private Documents

CVs, application documents and other information submitted for administrative review are treated as private information.

Clients will not have access to a professional's private CV or confidential application information unless the professional has expressly authorized such disclosure or applicable law requires it.

06

Data Security

VORVENA will take reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect personal information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse or disclosure.

However, no internet-based platform can guarantee absolute security.

07

Information Sharing

VORVENA does not intend to make private user information publicly available.

Information may be shared with service providers or other parties where reasonably necessary to operate the platform, process services, maintain security, comply with legal obligations or facilitate an authorized project.

Public professional information may be visible to clients and other visitors according to the platform's profile settings.

08

Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have rights relating to your personal information, including requesting access, correction, deletion or clarification about how your information is used.

Requests may be reviewed and handled according to applicable legal requirements and VORVENA's operational policies.

YOUR PRIVACY MATTERS.

VORVENA is committed to building a professional community where people can connect and work while maintaining appropriate control over their personal information.
