COMMUNITY GUIDELINES.

VORVENA is built around skilled people, professionalism and real opportunities. These guidelines help us maintain a safe, respectful and professional community.

Last Updated: August 2026

ON THIS PAGE

01

Respect Everyone

Treat professionals, clients, administrators and other members with respect.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, bullying and abusive behavior are not accepted within VORVENA.

02

Be Honest

Members must provide truthful and accurate information when applying, creating profiles or communicating with clients.

Do not claim skills, qualifications, experience or work that you do not genuinely possess.

False documents, fake identities and misleading information may result in account suspension or removal.

03

Maintain Professionalism

VORVENA members represent themselves and the wider community whenever they interact with clients.

Be punctual, communicate clearly, respect agreements and deliver work according to the agreed requirements.

04

Work Standards

Professionals should only accept work they have the ability and availability to complete.

Submitted work should be original, properly licensed or otherwise legally permitted for use.

Plagiarism, stolen work and unauthorized use of another person's intellectual property are prohibited.

05

Communication

Communication between clients and professionals should remain respectful, clear and professional.

Do not use VORVENA to send spam, scams, malicious links or misleading messages.

Important project and payment communications may be reviewed by administrators where necessary to manage disputes, security or platform operations.

06

Respect Privacy

Do not request, publish or distribute another member's private information without appropriate permission.

Private CVs, contact information, application records and other confidential information must not be shared publicly.

07

Prohibited Actions

The following activities are not permitted:

  • Fraud or scams
  • Impersonation
  • Fake qualifications or documents
  • Harassment or threats
  • Plagiarism or stolen work
  • Malicious or harmful activity
  • Unauthorized access to accounts
  • Illegal activities
  • Attempts to bypass VORVENA's administrative process for jobs
08

Enforcement

VORVENA administrators may investigate reports of guideline violations.

Depending on the seriousness of an issue, actions may include a warning, removal of content, temporary suspension or permanent removal from the community.

Serious violations may be reported to appropriate authorities where required by law.

BUILD WITH RESPECT.

VORVENA works best when everyone brings professionalism, honesty and respect to the community.
