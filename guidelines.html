COMMUNITY GUIDELINES

BUILD A COMMUNITY
WORTH BEING PART OF.

VORVENA is built around talent, professionalism, respect, and meaningful opportunities. These guidelines help keep the community safe and valuable for everyone.

01 02 03 04 05 06 07 08
01

BE HONEST

Members must provide truthful and accurate information when creating an account, submitting a profile, or communicating with clients and administrators.

Do not misrepresent your skills, experience, qualifications, location, identity, or professional background.

02

USE YOUR OWN WORK

Only submit your own CV, profile photograph, portfolio, work samples, and professional information.

Do not present another person's work as your own or use someone else's identity, portfolio, or professional information without permission.

03

RESPECT EVERYONE

Treat other members, clients, administrators, and collaborators with professionalism and respect.

Harassment, threats, bullying, abusive behaviour, discrimination, and targeted hostility are not permitted within the VORVENA community.

04

NO SCAMS OR FRAUD

VORVENA must not be used to deceive, scam, defraud, or manipulate other people.

This includes fake job opportunities, fraudulent payments, impersonation, phishing, misleading offers, or intentionally providing false information.

05

KEEP PROFESSIONAL COMMUNICATION

Communication between professionals, clients, and administrators should remain professional and relevant to the work or opportunity being discussed.

Avoid spam, unwanted promotional messages, inappropriate content, and behaviour that disrupts the community.

06

OPPORTUNITIES & APPROVAL

Becoming a VORVENA member does not guarantee employment, clients, contracts, projects, income, or any specific professional outcome.

Opportunities received through VORVENA are handled according to the platform's administrative process.

07

PAYMENT & PROJECTS

For approved jobs arranged through VORVENA, the agreed project payment is handled through the platform's administrative process.

Under the platform's current payment structure, 30% is retained by VORVENA for the platform's administrative/service role, while 70% goes to the professional completing the approved job.

Project scope, payment terms, cancellations, refunds, disputes, and repeat work are subject to the applicable project agreement.

08

PROTECT THE COMMUNITY

If you notice suspicious activity, scams, impersonation, harassment, fraudulent behaviour, or other serious violations, report them to the VORVENA administration team.

VORVENA may review reported activity and take appropriate action where community rules are violated.
