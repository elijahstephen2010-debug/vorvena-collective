Acceptance of Terms

02

Community Membership

VORVENA is a professional community that connects skilled individuals with potential clients and opportunities.

Submitting an application does not automatically guarantee membership. Applications are reviewed by the VORVENA administration team.

VORVENA reserves the right to approve or reject applications based on the information provided and the needs of the community.

03

Professional Profiles

Approved professionals may receive a profile on the VORVENA platform.

Only information intended for public display will appear on a professional's public profile.

Private information such as CVs, passwords, application details and administrative records will not be publicly displayed.

Members are responsible for ensuring that the information they provide is accurate and up to date.

04

Job Opportunities

Job opportunities introduced through VORVENA are subject to the platform's administrative process.

Professionals should not begin work on a VORVENA-referred project until the required project and payment arrangements have been confirmed.

VORVENA may facilitate communication between clients and professionals but does not guarantee that every member will receive work.

05

Payments & Platform Fee

When a professional receives a job through VORVENA, the agreed project payment will be subject to the platform's applicable payment structure.

30% VORVENA
70% Professional

The current standard structure is that 30% of the applicable project payment goes to VORVENA for administration, platform operations and related services, while 70% goes to the professional.

The exact payment process, timing, responsibilities and handling of cancellations or disputes will be communicated before a project begins.

The 30% platform share does not represent a membership or application fee.

06

Community Conduct

Members are expected to behave professionally and respectfully when communicating with other members, clients and administrators.

Fraud, harassment, impersonation, submission of false information, plagiarism, scams or other harmful activities are not permitted.

Members must not use VORVENA to participate in illegal activities.

07

Privacy & Information

VORVENA may collect information required to operate the platform, review applications, create profiles and facilitate professional opportunities.

We will take reasonable steps to protect private information and limit access to information that is not intended for public display.

Clients will only be able to see information that VORVENA has designated as public on a professional's profile.

For more information, please review our Privacy Policy.

Account Suspension & Termination

VORVENA may suspend or terminate an account where there is a serious violation of these terms, community guidelines, fraudulent activity or behavior that may harm the community.

Members may also request to leave the community, subject to any existing project or payment obligations.
